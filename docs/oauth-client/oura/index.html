kuro-kakaritsuke

A small personal tool · not a public service

kuro-kakaritsuke is used only by its owner. It reads the owner's own Oura Ring data and writes the owner a one-line note each morning. It is not offered to anyone else.

Privacy Policy · Terms of Service

kuro-kakaritsuke（クロのかかりつけ）は、持ち主本人だけが、自分の Oura Ring のデータを読んで毎朝の一行をもらうために使う小さな個人用のツールです。公開サービスではありません。
