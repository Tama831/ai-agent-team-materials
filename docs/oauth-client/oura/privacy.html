Privacy Policy

kuro-kakaritsuke · Last updated: 5 October 2026

  • kuro-kakaritsuke is used only by its owner, with the owner's own Oura account, and reads only the owner's own data through the Oura API (daily summaries of sleep, readiness and activity, heart rate, workouts, tags and sessions).
  • The data is copied once a day to a private server operated by the owner, and is used for one purpose: to compose a short daily note for the owner.
  • No data is shared with, sold to, or transferred to any third party, and it is not used for advertising, profiling, or training AI models.
  • The data stays on the owner's private server for as long as the owner wants it, and the owner can delete it at any time.
  • Access can be revoked at any time by removing the application from the connected applications in the Oura account settings, or by deleting the stored tokens on the owner's server.
